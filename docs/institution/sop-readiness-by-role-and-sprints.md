# SOP — Cohort readiness by career role, and preparation sprints

> What this is: the procedure placement officers, administrators and department heads follow to see
> how their students stand against one career role's essential requirements, and to run a
> time-boxed preparation sprint for the students with a gap. Why it exists: Appendix 3 INST-19;
> journey J11. Built by INST-17. Written 2026-10-01 against base `719eb34`. Design:
> `docs/architecture/preparation-sprints-table-design.md`.

## Who and where

| Role | Where |
|---|---|
| Administrator, owner | **Career Readiness** |
| Placement officer | **Readiness** (below the monthly report) |
| Department head | **Readiness** — their department only |
| Faculty | Not available (lecturers use [Learning Resources](sop-faculty-learning.md)) |

## A. Read the cohort view

1. **Cohort readiness by career role**: choose a catalogue role, or the role behind one of your live
   campus drives, and a **Month (ended)**. The current month is refused: figures stand at the end of a
   whole month that has ended.
2. For each **essential requirement** you see how many students are *Met*, *Partly there*, *Not yet*,
   *Too little evidence* or *No evidence yet*, with gaps and unknowns.
3. Any group under five reads `<5`; if the whole cohort is under five, every figure is withheld. No
   student is ever named, and there is no score or percentage.

## B. Run a sprint

1. On a requirement with published content, **Run a sprint**: *What to practise* (a practice set or
   lesson), *Length (days)* 7 to 42, *Everyone in your scope* or one department, batch or section,
   optionally *Also include students with no evidence or too little evidence*, a **Reason** (preparing
   for a campus drive, a gap in an essential requirement, placement season preparation) and an
   optional note to students. **Start the sprint**.
2. You are told how many students it reached (`<5` under five) — never who. At most 500 students; choose
   a smaller unit if refused.
3. Students with the gap see the work on **Learning → From your college** with its end date, and are
   told in the app.

## C. Follow a sprint

1. **Preparation sprints** lists each sprint: reached, *Now meet it*, *New evidence, not yet met*,
   *Nothing new yet*, and work done. First figures appear the day after it starts; progress is counted
   up to the start of each day from the students' own evidence.
2. To stop it early: **End early…** with a reason (no longer needed, started in error, replaced by
   another sprint). Only the person who started it, an owner or an administrator can end it. The work
   leaves the students' Learning pages.

## Never do

- Never present these bands as a score or a ranking of students.
- Never try to identify who is in a `<5` group by narrowing the unit.
- Never promise an employer that a sprint makes students "ready".

## Gaps

- The view has no unit filter (a sprint can be narrowed by unit).
- A practice sprint's "work done" counts finished steps; *Now meet it* moves only with lesson or
  assessment evidence.
- Sprints are started and ended on the web only; Android shows the sprints and one role's view
  read-only.
- Notices are in-app only.
