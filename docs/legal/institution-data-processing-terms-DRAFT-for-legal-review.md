## Proposed text

**ONE data-processing terms for colleges — draft for legal review**

These terms apply to every college that uses ONE, and to everyone who acts for the college in ONE. An
owner of the college's ONE workspace accepts them on the college's behalf.

**1. Roles.** [Legal review: the college's role and AI8HT's role for each category of record under the
DPDP Act, 2023 — for example, the college as Data Fiduciary for its roster, marks and staff notes, and
AI8HT's role in holding them; and the separate position for data a student adds to their own ONE
account.]

**2. What the college's records are.** In ONE, the college keeps: its structure (departments,
programmes, batches and sections), its roster of students, its staff and their assignments, its
assessments and the results, the actions and notes its staff record, its announcements, its settings
and calendar, and its decisions on academic records students submit for verification.

**3. What stays the student's.** A student's ONE account, and what they add to it — their documents,
applications, learning activity, readiness, messages, and the choices they make about what the college
may see — are the student's. The college sees an application only when the student shares it
(ADR-019), a document only when the student shares that version (ADR-027), and never offer pay
(ADR-024). [Legal review: how this division is expressed contractually.]

**4. Who at the college sees what.** Staff see only the students in their scope (department, batch,
section or teaching assignment). Counselling notes are readable only by their author, the college's
owners and admins, and staff added to that student's counselling case. Every export is recorded with
who, when and why.

**5. Instructions and purpose.** [Legal review: processing only on the college's documented
instructions; the purposes permitted.]

**6. Security.** [Legal review: the security commitments to state, and how they are evidenced.]

**7. Sub-processors.** [Legal review: the list of sub-processors (hosting, email delivery) and the
notice required before a change. The hosting provider is not yet contracted.]

**8. Breaches.** [Legal review: notification to the college and to the Data Protection Board — time
limits and content.]

**9. Requests from students.** A student can export and delete their own ONE data from their account.
A request that concerns the college's records (for example, a counselling note) is the college's to
answer; ONE lets owners and admins erase one note with a reason. [Legal review: assistance obligations
and timelines.]

**10. Changes to these terms.** When these terms change in a way that matters, an owner must accept
the new version before the college adds more students to ONE. Nothing already in place is paused.

**11. Leaving ONE.** When the college's agreement ends:
- AI8HT records a notice. While the notice is open, the college's owner can download the college's
  records as files: structure, roster, staff and assignments, assessments and results, staff actions,
  and verification decisions. Counselling notes are included only if the owner asks for them with a
  reason, and that request is recorded.
- AI8HT then ends the college's use of ONE: staff accounts lose access to the college, students' links
  with the college end, and each student is told.
- For 90 days after that, the exit can be reversed. After 90 days, and only with the approval of a
  second person at AI8HT, the college's records listed in clause 2 are deleted, except the names of its
  departments, programmes, batches and sections. Records under a legal hold are not deleted until the
  hold ends.
- Students keep their ONE accounts and everything in clause 3. A result the college verified stays
  verified on the student's record.
- [Legal review: the 90-day period (decision 10.2 #10, "subject to legal review"); certification of
  deletion; records AI8HT must keep by law; what happens to campus drives run with employers.]

**12. [Legal review: governing law, grievance officer and contact, liability, term and termination, and
the relationship to any separate written agreement with the college.]**
