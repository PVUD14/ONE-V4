## Proposed notice text

**Let verified employers find me (optional).** If you agree, verified employers using ONE can find a card about
you when they search the talent pool. The card shows only what you choose below: the skills you have shown on
ONE, with the evidence you tick (what kind of evidence, where it came from, its date and whether you did it
without help); your branch; your graduation year; and the cities you would like to work in. It never shows
your name, email, phone, photo, college, marks, scores or readiness.

An employer can invite you to apply to one of its jobs. Nothing about you is shared with that employer unless
you apply; if you apply, they see what you choose to share with your application, as with any other job. Once
you have applied, that employer may be able to tell that the application came from the card they invited.

You can block particular employers, change what your card shows, or leave the talent pool at any time from
your Profile. Leaving takes effect immediately: employers stop finding you from their next search. ONE records
each search, each card an employer opens and each invitation. You can see the employers who opened your card
in your data export.

**Your rights.** You can see, export, correct or erase your data and withdraw this choice at any time.
