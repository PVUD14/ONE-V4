# Frontend role portal matrix

This matrix maps the product-facing role portals implemented in the frontend preview to the repository role model and current backend enforcement posture.

Role selection in `/demo` is not authentication and does not create a principal, token, membership or backend permission.

| Portal family | Product-facing role | Canonical backend/domain role | Enforcement status | Tenant/scope | Allowed preview operations | Read-only/protected data | Forbidden operations | Dashboard | Visual theme |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Student | Student | `STUDENT` | Backend enforced | Self + active institution relationship | Student-owned profile, drafts, metadata, saved roles, learning progress | Calculated readiness, verified academic records, employer decisions | Change verification or employer-owned terms | `/student` | Navy / blue |
| Institution | Placement Officer | `PLACEMENT_ADMIN` enforced in service; `PLACEMENT_OFFICER` domain-model role | Partially backend enforced | Institution placement scope | Interventions, assessments, opportunity recommendations, placement coordination | Student consent, employer offer terms, verified academic data | Override identity, issue offers | `/institution/placement-officer` | Emerald / green |
| Institution | Lecturer / Faculty | `FACULTY_LECTURER` | Partially backend enforced | Assigned students / department scope | Draft assessments, student tasks, faculty notes, evidence requests, learning resources, sessions | Student private documents, employer decisions, readiness formula | Edit employer decisions or platform roles | `/institution/faculty` | Royal blue |
| Institution | Career Counselor | `CAREER_COUNSELOR` | Domain-model only | Assigned student cases / counseling scope | Sessions, private notes, follow-ups, career path recommendations, interventions, resources, outcomes | Employer decisions, academic source records outside consent | Expose private counselor notes to employers | `/institution/career-counselor` | Cyan / teal |
| Institution | Department Head | `DEPARTMENT_HEAD` | Partially backend enforced | Department scope | Faculty workload, department interventions, assessment plan review, meetings, saved dashboards, notes | Student-owned identity, employer offer terms | Edit Student-owned profile or issue offers | `/institution/department-head` | Forest green |
| Institution | Institution Analyst | `INSTITUTION_ANALYST` | Domain-model only | Institution analytics scope | Analysis filters, saved views, preview reports, chart configuration, export request presentation, annotations | Source records, verification decisions, placement decisions | Edit source records or backend authorization | `/institution/analyst` | Violet |
| Employer | Recruiter | `RECRUITER` | Backend enforced | Employer scope + consented applicants | Draft opportunities, shortlisting, recruiter stage movement, interview/assessment requests, offer drafts, notes, talent pool | Student private evidence, institution-only notes, other employers data | Bypass consent or view unrelated students | `/employer/recruiter` | Amber / orange |
| Employer | Hiring Manager | `HIRING_MANAGER` | Domain-model only | Hiring team / assigned requisitions | Requisition drafts, shortlist approvals, interview panels, evaluations, offer requests, hiring notes, candidate stage changes | Student private evidence, recruiter admin settings | Issue platform permissions or bypass consent | `/employer/hiring-manager` | Crimson / rose |
| Employer | Interviewer | `INTERVIEWER` | Domain-model only | Assigned interviews only | Candidate brief, draft scorecards, interview notes, preview feedback, prep completion | Offer decisions, unauthorized candidate data | Issue offers or change requisitions | `/employer/interviewer` | Gold / ochre |
| Operations | Platform Administrator / Operations Administrator | `OPERATIONS_ADMIN` | Backend enforced | Platform operations scope | Organization review, operational notes, queue status, support cases, preview feature flags, event acknowledgement, saved views | Secrets, tokens, production billing effects, real privileged mutations | Expose credentials, fake production health, weaken authorization | `/operations/admin` | Graphite / slate |

## Backend enforcement notes

- `apps/api/src/domain/identity/models.ts` contains the broad product/domain roles, including `PLACEMENT_OFFICER`, `CAREER_COUNSELOR`, `INSTITUTION_ANALYST`, `HIRING_MANAGER`, and `INTERVIEWER`.
- `apps/api/src/application/identity-organization/services.ts` currently enforces a narrower `OrganizationRole` set: platform admin roles, institution owner/admin/placement admin/department head/faculty lecturer, and employer owner/admin/recruiter.
- Therefore, several role portals are intentionally marked Domain-model only or Partially backend enforced. Their frontend actions are session-local previews and must not be represented as production authorization.
