## Proposed notice text

**Personalise my learning (optional).** If you agree, ONE looks at how you answer practice questions
— which ones you get right, how long you take and when you ask for a hint — to choose what you
practise next and to show which skills you have shown. If you do not agree, you still get the same
lessons and practice, in the same order as everyone else. You can turn this off at any time in
Settings → Privacy; ONE then stops personalising from that moment. What you have already done stays in
your record. [Proposed addition, INST-15, for legal review:] If you study at a college that uses ONE,
the lecturers who teach your section can see the skills you are stuck on and the skills due for
review; common mistakes are shown to them only as class totals of five or more students. They never
see a score.

**AI tutor (optional).** If you agree, when you ask the tutor for help, the question you are working on
and your answer so far are sent to our AI provider to write a hint. The tutor never gives you the
answer outright. [Provider name, location and retention to be confirmed at publication — AI inference
is on hold until it runs through ONE's managed-inference setup.] Your data is not used to train the
provider's models. You can turn this off at any time.

**Improve ONE (optional).** If you agree, your practice answers may be included, with your name and
anything that identifies you removed, in the data ONE uses to check and improve its questions and
learning engine. If you do not agree, or withdraw later, your answers are left out of every future use.

**Age.** ONE is for people aged 18 or over. If you are under 18, please do not create an account.
[Under-18 flow pending a verifiable parental-consent process.]

**Your rights.** You can see, export, correct or erase your data and withdraw any of these choices at
any time from Settings → Privacy. [Grievance officer contact to be inserted at publication.]
