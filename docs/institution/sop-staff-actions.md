# SOP — Interventions, assigned tasks, faculty notes and meetings

> What this is: the procedure college staff follow to record and track the support they give
> students — interventions, assigned tasks, faculty notes, verification reviews and meetings. Why it
> exists: Appendix 3 INST-19 asks for an SOP for every shipped module; these "staff actions" shipped
> before the institution plan (pre-plan) and had none. Supplementary UAT script I-W. INST-02 put them
> under the one scope rule; INST-11 split counselling notes off into their own restricted type
> ([counselling SOP](sop-counselling-notes.md)). Written 2026-10-01 against base `719eb34`.

## Where

| Record | Where |
|---|---|
| Intervention | **Interventions** (administrator, department head, faculty) |
| Assigned task | **Assigned Tasks** (faculty) |
| Faculty note | **Student Detail** (faculty) — titled *Faculty Notes* |
| Verification review note | **Academic Verification**, second tab (faculty) |
| Meeting | **Meetings** (department head), **Calendar** (faculty) |

Every list shows only students in your scope. Staff-only records are never shown to the student.

## Steps

1. **Create** (intervention: **Assign Intervention**): choose the student (only students in your scope
   are offered; a meeting may have no student), a title, a due date or the meeting's start and end.
   You own the record.
2. Save it as a draft, then move it through its states with the buttons shown — **Submit for review**,
   **Approve**, **Reject**, **Start**, **Complete**, **Cancel**, **Archive**. Lecturers cannot approve.
3. Before completing or cancelling, add the review or outcome note the screen asks for.
4. If someone else changed the record, refresh and try again (*This record has changed…*).
5. **Needs attention now** on Interventions lists students who have not attempted an assigned
   assessment — a starting point, not a judgement.

## Never do

- Never write counselling detail in a faculty note or an intervention — use a
  [counselling note](sop-counselling-notes.md), which has restricted readers.
- Never put marks, health or family details in a title: titles are shown in lists.

## Gaps

- A faculty note is readable by every staff role whose scope includes the student (see the security
  review, INST-SR-08).
- Faculty **Student Detail** is a notes list, not a full single-student view (screen inventory).
- Web only; Android has no staff-action screens.
