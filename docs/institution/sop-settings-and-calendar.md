# SOP — College settings, grading scales and the academic calendar

> What this is: the procedure college administrators follow to keep the college profile, each
> programme's grading scale and the academic calendar, and how other staff use their own
> settings. Why it exists: Appendix 3 INST-19 (IM1 slice). Built by INST-05. Written 2026-09-26
> against base `6864332`.

## Who and where

| Role | Where | Can |
|---|---|---|
| Owner, administrator | **Settings** | Edit the college profile, grading scales and calendar |
| Department head | **Settings** | Read the college settings; set own notification preferences |
| Faculty | **Profile**, **Settings**, **Calendar** | See own staff profile and teaching; set preferences; see the calendar beside meetings |
| Placement officer | — | No Settings item |

## A. College profile

1. **Settings → College profile**: *About* and the *placement office email* → **Save college
   profile**.
2. *Verified details* (legal name, address, website and the like) are read-only; ONE Operations
   changes them after checking.

## B. Grading scales

1. **Grading scales** → for each programme choose its scale (and pass mark where the scale has
   one) → **Save scale**.
2. A programme with no scale shows **NA** everywhere a scale is needed. ONE never guesses.
3. **Remove scale** needs a reason: `WRONG_SCALE`, `PROGRAMME_CLOSED`, `REGULATION_CHANGED`.

## C. Academic calendar

1. **Academic calendar** → add each term or event with its dates; **Edit** to correct.
2. **Archive** instead of deleting, with `CANCELLED`, `DUPLICATE` or `CORRECTION`.
3. Faculty see current and upcoming entries on their Calendar (and on the Android home).

## D. Personal notification preferences (every staff member)

**Settings → Notification preferences → Save preferences**. ONE sends staff no reminder emails
yet; the screen says so.

## Never do

- Never set a scale "to make numbers appear"; an NA is honest.

## Gaps

- No logo (needs the file store).
- *Placement policies* and *Data-processing terms* are shown as "not built yet" (EMP-11/INST-12,
  INST-18).
- Students do not see the academic calendar yet; teaching assignments use a free-text term, not
  the calendar.
