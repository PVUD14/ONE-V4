01

How this is sorted, and what was decided

A plan is only useful if the boundary between “this will be done” and “this needs someone else” is drawn honestly. Everything below carries one of four marks, and nothing is marked more optimistically than it deserves.

Delivered built, tested, on both web and phone, with the design documents the repository rules require — Gated buildable, but it crosses a line that is not mine to cross — Partway real progress is possible and the stopping point is stated — Not mine needs access, money or people.

What happened. A1 to A5 and the housekeeping ran straight through, then B1. The public profile shipped with ADR-031 recording that ONE now has one unauthenticated route — which belongs in a security review rather than being discovered in one. Photos were added to the first version at your request, which meant retiring the phone’s device-only photo so the two clients stop disagreeing. Billing stays closed: ADR-022 stands until a paying customer with a date, or a decision to sell to students directly.

02

The work that is finished

A1

Feedback reaches the rejected candidate Delivered

The highest value on the list, and cheaper than it looked: applications.rejection_reason_code already exists and is already written every time an employer rejects someone, alongside a full state history. Nobody has ever shown it to the student.

What is missing is a controlled vocabulary instead of free text, a release decision so the employer chooses what the candidate sees, a student-facing route, and the link from a released reason to the roadmap — so a rejection stops being a dead end and becomes the most useful signal in the system.

A4

Alumni ask their old college to verify Delivered

The verification engine already accepts people who are no longer enrolled — the queue is scoped by which institution issued the qualification and never asks whether the person is still a student. This builds the request path and the college’s queue for requests from people who are not on their roster.

This is the college-facing half, and it is useful on its own. It does not include self-registration, which is held in section 04.

03

The one that needed permission, and the one still held

B1

A profile the student can take outside ONE Delivered

Today there is not one unauthenticated route in the entire system. That is a real security property, and a shareable profile link ends it — which is why it needed a decision rather than a judgement call. It is now authorised.

Scoped deliberately: the link is revocable and expiring, it shows only what the student chose to share, verified and self-reported items are visibly distinguished, and the readiness band is excluded. An ADR records the decision and what was rejected, as the repository requires.

B2

Billing Held by decision

Not started. ADR-022 defers it until the pilot completes and names its own trigger — a paying customer with a date, or a decision to sell to students directly. Neither has happened, so the decision stands and the largest unbuilt area stays unbuilt on purpose.

When it reopens: the domain, schema and entitlement checks are mine. Wiring an actual payment provider is not — that needs merchant credentials.

04

Where I stop short, and why

C1

Speaking and interview practice Partway

Buildable now: the practice session and its recording; the band-with-categories schema carrying model and prompt version from the outset; CHECK constraints making a practice result structurally invisible to employers, exactly as readiness already is; and the microphone quality gate — which needs no model at all, being signal analysis in the browser, and which is the fairness mechanism the entire feature rests on. Poor audio must yield “we could not assess this”, never a low band.

Stops before

Any provider integration or model-dependent assessment code, which the repository holds until cloud access exists. When it arrives, what remains is one model call into a schema and a privacy boundary that are already built and already tested.

C2

A front door for people not at a college Partway

Buildable now: an independent-learner account, the onboarding around it, and honest labelling that distinguishes a self-reported claim from an institution-verified one.

Stops before

Registration itself, which is identity-provider configuration and needs cloud access. Everything can be ready and waiting; it cannot go live without that.

05

Not mine at all

Staff two-factor

Decided and already handled by the application, but it needs a separate staff sign-in pool — cloud access. Today’s weakest link, and first in the queue when that access arrives.

Independent penetration test

An external party should attack this, precisely because everything claimed about its security so far is our own assessment of our own work.

Content stock and reviewers

A model can draft; it cannot carry authority. One named subject expert per field, one field at a time — depth in three beats shallowness across thirty.

AI features

Held until managed inference is reachable. The orchestration is built and provider-neutral; one adapter remains.

06

What happens after this

  1. Write the content. The desk exists and two people must sign off before a student sees anything. What it needs now is somebody who knows a field, writing into it. Nothing else on this list unlocks as much.

  2. Finish the front door. Retroactive verification shipped; self-signup is the missing half, and it is what lets a person with no institution use any of this. ONE’s side is straightforward; registration waits on cloud access.

  3. Get cloud access. It is the single blocker on staff two-factor, every AI feature, and the half of speaking practice that listens. Three separate items, one obstacle.

  4. Commission a penetration test before real student data arrives at scale — with more surface to look at now that one unauthenticated route exists.

  5. Reopen billing when there is a paying customer with a date. That is the trigger ADR-022 names, and nothing about it has changed.

Standing rules this work obeyed. Every item ships to the Android app in the same piece of work, and any item with genuinely no phone counterpart will be named as such rather than quietly skipped. A table carrying real consequences gets its design written into docs/architecture/ before its migration. Migrations were only added, never edited — 0035, 0036 and 0071 were used; 0016 and 0037–0039 remain free. Nothing invented ever reaches a screen a real user can see: a missing source renders NA or 0 rather than a plausible figure. And readiness stays a band, never a number, and never reaches an employer.
