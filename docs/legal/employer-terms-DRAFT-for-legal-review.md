## Proposed text

**ONE employer terms — draft for legal review**

These terms apply to every organisation that uses ONE to hire students and graduates, and to everyone
who acts for that organisation in ONE. An owner of the organisation accepts them on its behalf.

**1. Candidate data is for this hiring process only.** When a student applies to one of your jobs, you
may see what they chose to share with that application. You may use it only to consider them for that
job and to communicate with them about it. You may not use it for any other purpose, add it to another
database, or contact the candidate about anything else.

**2. Access ends when the process ends.** Your access to a candidate ends when their application is
closed — when you hire or reject them, when they withdraw, or when they delete their ONE account — and
when an access period set for that application runs out. ONE stops showing you the candidate from that
moment. Résumé downloads are single-use links that expire after one minute.
[Legal review: whether a fixed retention period after closure is required or permitted, and how long.]

**3. What you keep outside ONE.** If you download a candidate's résumé, you are responsible for it.
Delete it when the hiring process for that job ends, and in any case when the candidate asks.
[Legal review: the employer's own retention obligation and period under the DPDP Act, 2023.]

**4. No onward sharing.** Do not share candidate data with anyone outside your organisation's hiring
team for that job, including recruiters, agencies or group companies, unless the candidate has agreed.
[Legal review: processors acting for the employer.]

**5. Never ask a candidate for money.** Do not ask applicants for any payment, deposit, fee, training
charge or equipment purchase at any stage. ONE refuses to publish job text that asks for one, and a
candidate can report a request made any other way.

**6. Fair hiring.** Do not select, filter or reject candidates on gender, religion, caste, age,
disability, marital status, region of origin, or anything that stands in for them. Eligibility in ONE
uses only the rule types the job form offers. Every rejection is a decision a person makes.

**7. Messages stay in ONE.** Contact candidates through the application's conversation in ONE. ONE keeps
message text for 180 days after your access to that candidate ends, then erases it, unless it is needed
for an open trust and safety case or a legal hold.

**8. Reports and your right of reply.** A candidate can report a job, your organisation or a message.
A report opens a case; it is not a penalty. If ONE operations ask you to respond, you will see a summary
of the concern and can reply. You will not be told who reported it. If ONE operations take action, you
can appeal the decision once.

**9. Changes to these terms.** When these terms change in a way that matters, an owner must accept the
new version before your organisation publishes another job. Jobs already live stay live.

relationship to any separate written agreement with the employer.]**
